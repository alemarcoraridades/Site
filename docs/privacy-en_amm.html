1. Service Description

The application Alemarco Motor offers the following functionalities:

  • Maintenance Control: Management of critical and non-critical maintenance based on a maintenance catalog for engines (initially AP, with future expansion to other models).
  • Diagnosis: Symptom-based diagnostic tool to assist in identifying problems.
  • Maintenance Help: Basic guidelines for performing maintenance.
  • Maintenance History: Recording and consulting the history of performed maintenance.
  • Speedometer (GPS): Speed measurement via GPS.
